# ICP Definition (Hard Filters)

> Who we target and who we exclude. Referenced by `01_source_leads.md`, `02_pretest_icp.md`, and `05_personalize.md`.

---

## Primary ICP

- **Industry:** Performance Marketing Agencies (paid media, CRO, lifecycle/email, Amazon ads, analytics/attribution consultancies)
- **Geography:** United States + Canada only
- **Company size:** 10–500 employees (hard cap at 500)

---

---

## Hard Exclusions

- Solo freelancers / solo consultants
- Holding companies / PE portfolio ops / umbrella entities
- Agencies that only do SEO / content / social (non-performance)
- Enterprise exec-chair/board style profiles
- Creative agencies, branding agencies, design agencies
- In-house / internal marketing teams
