# Copy Rules & Anti-AI Detection

> Rules for the cold-email sequence and the generated reply variables.

---

## Nick Framework Rule

Email 1 must follow this order:
1. personalization
2. identity
3. offer
4. CTA

If those parts do not flow in that order, the email is wrong.

---

## Cold Email Rules

- Optimize for interested replies, not booked meetings
- Keep Email 1 under 90 words
- Personalization should feel like a cold read, not praise
- Identity should answer who the sender is and why it matters
- Offer should be concrete and easy to picture
- CTA should be low-friction and give-first
- Follow-ups should feel like human pings, not fresh pitch stacks
- Use first-person singular only: `I`, never `we`
- Use in-group language when it is clearly grounded in the lead context
- A tiny casual imperfection is acceptable when it feels natural, but never in the subject line and never as a gimmick

---

## Subject Line Rules

- 1-4 words
- lowercase
- plausible as an internal or personal subject line
- no emojis
- no fake `Re:` or `Fwd:` outside genuine follow-up steps

---

## Proof Rules

- proof must match the prospect's lane
- use operational detail, not vague bragging
- no fake percentages
- no fake absolute outcome claims
- no client names unless the campaign brief explicitly allows them

---

## Banned Vocabulary

```text
delve, leverage, streamline, landscape, robust, seamless, cutting edge, game changer,
unlock, empower, foster, holistic, innovative, groundbreaking, pivotal, ever evolving,
harness, optimize, spearhead, elevate, navigate, amplify, reimagine, impressed,
unprecedented, transformative, synergy, disruptive, paradigm, revolutionize,
comprehensive, facilitate, utilize, endeavor, multifaceted, intricate, nuanced
```

---

## Banned Outreach Phrases

```text
i hope this email finds you well
just wanted to reach out
i came across your profile on linkedin
just bumping this
i noticed on linkedin
saw your headline
```

---

## Contractions

Use contractions everywhere. Examples:

- `don't`, not `do not`
- `can't`, not `cannot`
- `it's`, not `it is`
- `i'm`, not `i am`
- `i've`, not `i have`
- `i'll`, not `i will`
- `you're`, not `you are`

---

## Persuasion Principles

- Give first: cold email earns replies with value before time asks
- Micro commitments: reply first, meeting later
- Social proof: use matched proof, not vague success claims
- Authority: credibility should feel native, not bolted on
- Rapport: write like someone who understands the lane
- Shared identity: use the prospect's operating language
- Scarcity: use only when truthful and low-pressure
